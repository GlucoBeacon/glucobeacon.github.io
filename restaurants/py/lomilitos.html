We scored 18 entree-style items on Lomilito's menu for how well they fit a diabetes-friendly meal -- glycemic impact, carb and calorie load against a standard 60g-carb / 600-calorie budget, and how processed the item is. No restaurant in Paraguay is required by law to publish nutrition facts (Paraguay's Ley N° 7092/2023, the front-of-pack warning-label law for packaged foods, covers only packaged products manufactured, portioned, or sold for retail -- it doesn't mention or cover restaurants or menu items) -- and Lomilito's doesn't publish its own nutrition data. So the calories and carbs here are AI-estimated, based on real ingredients and standard preparation for each item -- the same estimation approach the AI Food Scan feature inside the GlucoBeacon app uses, not official published figures. How We Score It

Each item gets up to 30 points for glycemic impact (lower is better), 30 points for staying under a 60g carb budget, 15 points for staying under a 600-calorie budget, and 25 points for being minimally processed -- the same formula GlucoBeacon's in-app Restaurant Advisor uses, just run here against a generic budget instead of your own goals. A higher score means an easier fit into most diabetes meal plans; it isn't a guarantee for any individual, since real blood sugar response depends on portion size, what else you eat with it, and your own physiology. The Est. GI is an estimate based on food type and cooking method (no restaurant publishes real, lab-tested GI per item), not a lab-measured value.
