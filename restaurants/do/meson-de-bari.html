We scored 8 entree-style items on Mesón de Bari's menu for how well they fit a diabetes-friendly meal -- glycemic impact, carb and calorie load against a standard 60g-carb / 600-calorie budget, and how processed the item is. No restaurant in the Dominican Republic is required by law to publish nutrition facts (INDOCAL's NORDOM 53 / NORDOM 675 labeling standards explicitly exclude food prepared for immediate consumption in restaurants, covering only pre-packaged retail products) -- and Mesón de Bari doesn't publish its own nutrition data. How We Score It

Each item gets up to 30 points for glycemic impact (lower is better), 30 points for staying under a 60g carb budget, 15 points for staying under a 600-calorie budget, and 25 points for being minimally processed -- the same formula GlucoBeacon's in-app Restaurant Advisor uses, just run here against a generic budget instead of your own goals. A higher score means an easier fit into most diabetes meal plans; it isn't a guarantee for any individual, since real blood sugar response depends on portion size, what else you eat with it, and your own physiology. The Est. GI is an estimate based on food type and cooking method (no restaurant publishes real, lab-tested GI per item), not a lab-measured value.
