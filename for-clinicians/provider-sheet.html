GlucoBeacon is a diabetes self-management and education tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The A1C figure it shows is an estimate calculated from the patient’s own logged glucose readings, not a laboratory result. It is not a substitute for the care of a qualified health professional. Patient logs are stored on the patient’s device by default; GlucoBeacon does not receive them, and this program gives the clinic no access to patient data — only a redemption count for the code. There is no cost and no payment in either direction.
