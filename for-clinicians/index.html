GlucoBeacon for Clinicians

One code. 90 days of Pro for every patient you give it to.

GlucoBeacon is a diabetes self-management app your patients can use to keep one complete daily record between visits. Enroll your clinic and you get a single code that unlocks 90 days of Pro for anyone who enters it — long enough to build the habit and see a pattern or two — with no cost, no payment in either direction, and no access to patient data.

What the app does

A logging and education tool — not a treatment.

GlucoBeacon puts a patient’s glucose, medication, food, and activity in one place and helps them make sense of what they’ve recorded. It does not diagnose, does not adjust therapy, and does not replace the care you provide.

  • Logs glucose, medication, meals, activity, and fasting in a single daily view — mg/dL or mmol/L, either as first-class.
  • Estimates an A1C from the glucose readings the patient has logged — shown as an estimate, with the reading count it’s based on.
  • Surfaces patterns in the logged data — time-of-day trends, post-meal responses, streaks.
  • Scores restaurant menu items (132 chains, 4,600+ items) and 281 dietitian-reviewed recipes by their likely effect on blood glucose.
  • An AI food-photo estimate for meals that aren’t on a menu, plus barcode and pantry tools.
  • Fully localized in English, Spanish, Filipino, and Hindi — the whole app, not machine-translated.

On patient data. GlucoBeacon stores a patient’s logs on their own device by default. We do not receive them, and enrolling your clinic gives neither us nor you any access to a patient’s records. You can see how many times your clinic’s code has been redeemed — a count, nothing else.

How it works

Enroll once, then hand the code out however you like.

  1. Enroll your clinic

    Email us the details below. We create one code for your clinic — something like CLINIC-YOURNAME — and send it back with a printable provider sheet.

  2. Share it with patients

    Say it in a visit, print it on a handout, pin the provider sheet in the waiting room, or add it to your after-visit summary. However you already share resources.

  3. The patient redeems it

    On Android: they open GlucoBeacon and enter the code in Settings › Redeem Code. On iPhone: they tap a one-time link you share (Apple’s rules don’t allow an in-app code box on iOS). Both paths are on the provider sheet.

  4. They have 90 days to try it

    The trial runs 90 days from redemption. Near the end the app reminds them; they keep every log either way, and can subscribe if they want to keep the Pro features.

  5. You can check the count

    Ask us any time for how many redemptions your code has had, or to raise its cap. That number is all that exists — no patient identities, no data.

iPhone — one-time linkApp Store redemption

Patient taps the link on your provider sheet (or scans its QR) → the App Store opens → the 90-day trial is applied to their Apple ID. Each iPhone link is single-use; we send a block and refill it on request.

Questions

What clinicians ask.

Is this a medical device?

No. GlucoBeacon is a self-management and education tool. It does not diagnose, does not recommend or adjust treatment, and is not a substitute for clinical care. It carries a medical disclaimer in-app.

Do you get my patients’ data?

No. Logs are stored on the patient’s device by default. We receive a redemption count for your code and nothing else — no identities, no health data. Enrolling doesn’t give you access to their records either.

Is there any payment involved?

None, in either direction. You don’t pay to enroll, your patients don’t pay for the trial, and we don’t pay you. The clinician program has no commission component on purpose.

What happens after 90 days?

The patient keeps all of their logged data. Pro features return to the free tier. They can subscribe to keep them or continue on the free version — their choice, nothing is lost. The app reminds them a few days before the trial ends.

Why 90 days and not longer?

It’s a starter window, the way a medication sample is — enough to establish the habit and see whether it helps, without replacing a subscription for people who’d benefit from keeping it. Clinics running a class or a study cohort can ask for a longer code.

Can I get more redemptions, or a code for a specific program?

Yes. Ask and we’ll raise the cap, or issue a separate code for a class, study cohort, or department so you can track them apart.

Which countries and languages?

The app is on the App Store and Google Play in most countries, and fully localized in English, Spanish, Filipino, and Hindi. The clinic code works the same everywhere on Android; iPhone redemption links are issued per App Store region.
