Why this doesn’t carry the liability you might expect

This is a tool a payer, employer, or insurer buys for a population — not a mechanism for pricing any individual’s premium. No one’s price changes based on their personal glucose or medication data; the purchaser pays a flat per-activation fee for the group, and every activated member gets the identical entitlement regardless of what their data ends up showing.

That’s a deliberate design choice, not an accident: it keeps this entirely outside ACA community rating, GINA, and the still-unresolved ADA/GINA wellness-incentive question, because none of those govern a population-level purchasing decision — only individual-level pricing and underwriting. Member logs stay on the member’s device by default; GlucoBeacon does not receive claims-linkable PHI, and payment doesn’t buy access to it.
