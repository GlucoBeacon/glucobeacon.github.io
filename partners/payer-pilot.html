The opportunity

Members with diagnosed diabetes cost roughly 2.6× a matched non-diabetic member — about $12,000 a year in attributable claims (American Diabetes Association, 2022). A sustained one-point drop in A1C is associated with $430–$740 per member per year in avoided medical cost and about 37% fewer microvascular complications (U.S. claims analyses; UKPDS 35). Digital self-management tools move A1C 0.3–0.5 points in members who engage. This pilot measures whether GlucoBeacon does that in your population, on your claims data, with a spending cap and a full refund if it fails to move A1C.

What GlucoBeacon is

A diabetes self-management app that keeps a member’s glucose, medication, food, and activity in one daily record. Live on the App Store and Google Play; fully localized in English, Spanish, Filipino, and Hindi. It includes an A1C estimate from logged readings, pattern detection, a Restaurant Advisor scoring 4,600+ menu items across 132 chains, an AI food-photo estimate, and 281 dietitian-reviewed recipes. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat — the pathway to lower cost is better data, better daily decisions, and visible medication adherence.

Pilot design

Population~3,000–5,000 members with diagnosed diabetes (Type 2 primary), or a single employer group or clinic panel of similar size.
Intervention armMembers who opt in through a co-branded link in your member hub (or after-visit summary) receive a 12-month GlucoBeacon Pro entitlement. Opt-in only.
Control armA matched cohort drawn from your own claims — age, sex, baseline A1C, comorbidity index, region — not offered the app during the pilot, offered it afterward.
Duration6-month primary read; 12-month secondary read.
Primary endpointChange in A1C, intervention vs. control (difference-in-differences on your claims / HEDIS Comprehensive Diabetes Care data).
Secondary endpointsDiabetes-related ED visits and inpatient admissions; total cost of care PMPM; medication adherence (proportion of days covered, from pharmacy claims); engagement (activation rate, sustained-tracker rate at 6 months).
Who runs the analysisYour team or an independent third party, on your claims. GlucoBeacon supplies only aggregate engagement data.

Data & privacy

Member logs are stored on the member’s device by default. GlucoBeacon receives no claims-linkable PHI, and the pilot fee does not buy access to member data. All outcome analysis runs on your claims against the matched control. GlucoBeacon will execute a DUA or BAA as your compliance team requires. SOC 2 Type II is in progress; the pilot architecture — a co-branded link plus a signed deep link, with no systems integration — does not require it.

Commercials — capped and refundable

  • Price: one-time $30 per activated member (12-month Pro entitlement), invoiced monthly against actual activations. Volume / pilot discount available.
  • Cap: billed activations are capped at a number you set; nothing is charged for eligible members who do not opt in. A 4,000-activation cap is a maximum pilot spend of $120,000.
  • Performance guarantee: if the intervention cohort does not show a statistically meaningful A1C improvement versus control at the 6-month read, GlucoBeacon refunds 100% of activation fees.
