Light Pickled Fish Escabeche

30 min total · 4 servings

A real Puerto Rican pan-seared fish marinated in a tangy vinegar, onion, and pepper escabeche -- naturally light and low in carbs.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Escabeche is a vinegar-based marinade with onions and peppers, not a starch or sugar-based sauce, so it has essentially no carbohydrate content. Paired with a lean, pan-seared fish fillet, this dish delivers real tangy island flavor while staying close to zero glycemic impact.

Ingredients

  • 44 white fish fillets (such as red snapper or grouper)
  • 11 onion, thinly sliced
  • 11 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup120 ml white vinegar
  • 3 tbsp45 ml olive oil
  • 88 black peppercorns and 2 bay leaves

Instructions

  1. Season the fish with salt and pepper, and pan-sear in a little olive oil, about 3-4 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through.
  2. In a separate pan, sauté the onion and bell pepper briefly, about 3 minutes.
  3. Add the vinegar, remaining olive oil, peppercorns, and bay leaves, and simmer 3-4 minutes to make the escabeche.
  4. Pour the warm escabeche over the cooked fish and let it marinate at least 15 minutes before serving, chilled or at room temperature.
