Light Palak Paneer

35 min total · 4 servings

A real North Indian spinach and paneer curry, made with a lighter hand on cream and oil -- true palak paneer flavor, naturally low in carbs.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 10g of carbs per serving, it fits comfortably within most low-carb diabetes meal plans, and its estimated glycemic index of ~25 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 15g of protein per serving also helps slow digestion and blunt any post-meal rise.

Instructions

  1. Blanch the spinach in boiling water 2 minutes, then drain and blend into a smooth puree.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and sauté the onion, garlic, ginger, and green chili until soft, about 5 minutes.
  3. Add the tomato, turmeric, and garam masala, and cook 4-5 minutes until the tomato breaks down.
  4. Stir in the spinach puree and salt, and simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Add the paneer cubes and milk, and simmer 3-4 minutes until warmed through. Serve hot.
