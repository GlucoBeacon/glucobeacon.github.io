Ensalada Mixta

15 min total · 2 servings

A simple mixed salad with tomato, olives, and tuna -- real everyday Spanish lunch, light and satisfying.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

This salad is almost entirely protein and healthy fat from tuna, egg, and olive oil, with only a small amount of carbs from the vegetables -- one of the lowest-carb meals in the collection.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups120 g mixed greens
  • 11 tomato, sliced
  • 1/41/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup35 g Kalamata olives
  • 1 can1 can tuna in olive oil, drained
  • 22 hard-boiled eggs, quartered
  • 2 tbsp30 ml olive oil

Instructions

  1. Arrange the mixed greens on a plate or in a bowl.
  2. Top with the tomato, onion, olives, tuna, and hard-boiled egg.
  3. Whisk the olive oil, sherry vinegar, and salt for the dressing.
  4. Drizzle the dressing over the salad just before serving.
