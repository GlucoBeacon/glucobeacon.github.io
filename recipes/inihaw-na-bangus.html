Inihaw na Bangus

30 min total · 2 servings

Whole grilled milkfish stuffed with tomato and onion -- real Filipino grilled-fish tradition, simple and smoky.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

This is essentially a whole grilled fish with no starch at all -- carbs come only from the tomato and onion stuffing, making it one of the lowest-carb, highest-protein meals in this collection.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb680 g whole bangus (milkfish), butterflied and deboned
  • 11 tomato, diced
  • 1/21/2 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp30 ml calamansi or lime juice
  • 1 tbsp15 ml soy sauce

Instructions

  1. Marinate the fish in calamansi juice, soy sauce, pepper, and salt for 15 minutes.
  2. Stuff the cavity with the diced tomato and onion.
  3. Grill over medium-high heat, 6-7 minutes per side, until cooked through and slightly charred.
  4. Serve with extra calamansi and a side of vinegar dipping sauce.
