Dominican Green Salad

10 min total · 4 servings

A real, everyday Dominican side salad of lettuce, tomato, cucumber, and avocado with a light vinaigrette -- fresh and naturally low in carbs.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

This salad is built entirely from non-starchy vegetables and avocado, with no rice, bread, or added sugar, so it has almost no effect on blood sugar while adding real volume, freshness, and healthy fat to any Dominican meal.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups4 cups romaine lettuce, chopped
  • 22 tomatoes, diced
  • 11 cucumber, sliced
  • 11 avocado, diced
  • 1/41/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp30 ml olive oil and vinegar dressing

Instructions

  1. Combine the lettuce, tomato, cucumber, and red onion in a large bowl.
  2. Add the avocado and toss gently.
  3. Drizzle with the olive oil and vinegar dressing.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste and serve immediately.
