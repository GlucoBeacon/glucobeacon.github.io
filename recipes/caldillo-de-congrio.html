Chilean Fish Soup (Caldillo de Congrio)

40 min total · 4 servings

Chile's celebrated coastal fish soup, made famous by poet Pablo Neruda -- real congrio (or any firm white fish) simmered with tomato, carrot, and cilantro in a light broth.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

This soup is built on lean white fish and a light vegetable broth rather than cream or a heavy roux, so it's naturally low in carbs and fat while delivering a full serving of protein. The small amount of potato and carrot digest gradually within the broth instead of spiking blood sugar the way a starch-heavy stew would.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb675 g firm white fish fillets (congrio, cod, or halibut), cut into chunks
  • 11 small potato, cubed
  • 11 carrot, sliced
  • 11 onion, chopped
  • 22 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/4 cup10 g fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 5 cups1.2 L low-sodium fish or vegetable broth

Instructions

  1. In a large pot, sauté the onion until soft, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the broth, tomato, potato, and carrot, and bring to a boil.
  3. Simmer 15 minutes until the potato and carrot are tender.
  4. Gently add the fish chunks and simmer 8-10 more minutes until just cooked through.
  5. Stir in fresh cilantro, season with salt and pepper, and serve hot.
