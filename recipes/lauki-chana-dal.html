Lauki Chana Dal

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 28g of carbs per serving, it's worth pairing with protein or fat and watching portion size to keep the resulting blood sugar rise gradual, and its estimated glycemic index of ~30 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 8g of fiber per serving adds the same slowing effect.

Instructions

  1. Rinse the chana dal and add to a pot with the water and turmeric. Bring to a boil, then simmer 20 minutes until half-tender.
  2. Add the bottle gourd and simmer 12-15 more minutes until both the dal and gourd are tender.
  3. In a small pan, heat the oil and add the cumin seeds and garlic, and sauté until golden, about 2 minutes.
  4. Add the onion and tomato to the tempering pan and cook 4-5 minutes until soft.
  5. Pour the tempering over the dal, season with salt, and serve hot.
