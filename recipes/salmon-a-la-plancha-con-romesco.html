Salmón a la Plancha con Romesco

30 min total · 2 servings

Pan-seared salmon with a smoky almond-red pepper romesco sauce -- real Catalan flavor, rich in omega-3s.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Salmon is high in protein and omega-3 fats with virtually no carbs, and the romesco sauce adds flavor without added sugar -- one of the lowest-carb, most heart-healthy meals in this collection.

Ingredients

  • 2 fillets2 fillets (170 g each) salmon (6 oz each)
  • 11 roasted red bell pepper
  • 1/4 cup35 g almonds, toasted
  • 1 clove1 clove garlic
  • 1 tbsp15 ml sherry vinegar
  • 3 tbsp45 ml olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp2.5 ml smoked paprika

Instructions

  1. Blend the roasted red pepper, almonds, garlic, sherry vinegar, 2 tbsp olive oil, and smoked paprika into a smooth romesco sauce.
  2. Season the salmon with salt.
  3. Heat the remaining olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat.
  4. Sear the salmon 4-5 minutes per side until cooked through.
  5. Serve topped with the romesco sauce.
