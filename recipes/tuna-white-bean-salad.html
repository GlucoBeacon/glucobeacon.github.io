Tuna & White Bean Salad

10 min total · 2 servings

Canned tuna and cannellini beans tossed with red onion, parsley, and lemon.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 24g of carbs per serving, it's worth pairing with protein or fat and watching portion size to keep the resulting blood sugar rise gradual, and its estimated glycemic index of ~30 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 28g of protein per serving also helps slow digestion and blunt any post-meal rise, and 8g of fiber per serving adds the same slowing effect.

Ingredients

  • 2 5 oz can canned tuna in olive oil, drained
  • 1 cup cannellini beans
  • 1/4 cup red onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 whole lemon
  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

Instructions

  1. Combine the tuna, beans, and diced onion in a bowl.
  2. Dress with lemon juice, olive oil, and parsley.
  3. Toss gently and serve chilled or at room temperature.
