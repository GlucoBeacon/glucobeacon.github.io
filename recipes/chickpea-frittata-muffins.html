Chickpea Frittata Muffins

30 min total · 4 servings

Make-ahead egg muffins with chickpea flour, spinach, and feta.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

With only 5g of carbs per serving, it's a lighter-carb option without needing a strict low-carb framing, and its estimated glycemic index of ~35 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 9g of protein per serving also helps slow digestion and blunt any post-meal rise, and 1g of fiber per serving adds the same slowing effect.

Ingredients

  • 4 large4 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup60 ml chickpea flour
  • 1 cup235 ml baby spinach
  • 1/4 cup60 ml low-sodium feta cheese
  • 1/4 cup60 ml bell pepper
  • 1/4 tsp1 ml black pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease a muffin tin.
  2. Whisk eggs with chickpea flour until smooth.
  3. Stir in spinach, feta, and bell pepper.
  4. Divide among muffin cups and bake 18-20 minutes until set.
