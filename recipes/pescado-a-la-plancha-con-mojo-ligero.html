Grilled Fish with Light Mojo

27 min total · 4 servings

A pan-seared fish fillet topped with Cuba's real garlic-and-citrus mojo sauce -- classic Cuban flavor, naturally light and quick.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Mojo sauce -- garlic, sour orange or lime juice, and olive oil -- has essentially no carbohydrates on its own. Paired with a lean, pan-seared fish fillet instead of fried yuca or plantain, this dish delivers the full, real mojo flavor Cuban cooking is known for while staying close to zero glycemic impact.

Ingredients

  • 44 white fish fillets (such as tilapia or snapper)
  • 6 cloves6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/3 cup80 ml fresh sour orange or lime juice
  • 3 tbsp45 ml olive oil
  • 1 tsp1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/4 cup10 g fresh cilantro, chopped

Instructions

  1. Season the fish with salt and pepper, and pan-sear in a little olive oil over medium-high heat, about 3-4 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through.
  2. Meanwhile, heat the remaining olive oil in a small pan and sauté the garlic until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  3. Add the citrus juice and cumin, and simmer 2-3 minutes to make the mojo sauce.
  4. Pour the warm mojo sauce over the cooked fish, sprinkle with cilantro, and serve immediately.
