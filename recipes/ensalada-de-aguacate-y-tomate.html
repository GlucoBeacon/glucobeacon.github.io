Avocado and Tomato Salad

10 min total · 4 servings

A simple, real Colombian side salad of ripe avocado, tomato, and lime -- light, fresh, and naturally low in carbs.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Avocado is rich in healthy monounsaturated fat and fiber with almost no digestible carbs, and tomato is a low-GI vegetable, so this salad barely moves the needle on blood sugar while its fat and fiber content actually help slow the digestion of anything else on the plate.

Ingredients

  • 22 ripe avocados, sliced
  • 33 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1/41/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp30 ml fresh lime juice
  • 1 tbsp15 ml olive oil
  • 2 tbsp8 g fresh cilantro, chopped

Instructions

  1. Arrange the sliced avocado and tomato on a platter, alternating slices.
  2. Scatter the red onion over the top.
  3. Whisk together the lime juice and olive oil, then drizzle over the salad.
  4. Sprinkle with cilantro, salt, and pepper, and serve immediately.
