Chicken & Chickpea Tagine

45 min total · 4 servings

Chicken thighs braised with chickpeas, tomato, apricots, and warm spices.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 29g of carbs per serving, it's worth pairing with protein or fat and watching portion size to keep the resulting blood sugar rise gradual, and its estimated glycemic index of ~40 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 7g of fiber per serving adds the same slowing effect.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs
  • 1 1/2 cup chickpeas
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1/3 cup dried apricots
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 whole onion
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Instructions

  1. Brown the chicken in olive oil, then set aside.
  2. Sauté the onion with cinnamon and cumin until fragrant.
  3. Return the chicken to the pot with tomato, chickpeas, and apricots.
  4. Simmer covered for 25–30 minutes until the chicken is tender.
