Lentil Salad with Lean Chorizo

40 min total · 4 servings

A hearty lentil salad with a small amount of grilled chorizo for smoky flavor -- a real everyday Argentine lunch, built around a naturally slow-digesting legume.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Lentils have one of the lowest glycemic indexes of any staple food and are packed with fiber, so they digest slowly and help blunt blood sugar spikes. Keeping the chorizo as a small flavor accent rather than the main event keeps saturated fat in check while still delivering real smoky, savory taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups400 g cooked lentils
  • 3 oz85 g lean chorizo or grilled sausage, diced and browned
  • 11 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1/21/2 red onion, finely chopped
  • 1/4 cup10 g fresh parsley, chopped
  • 3 tbsp45 ml olive oil and red wine vinegar dressing

Instructions

  1. Brown the diced chorizo in a dry pan over medium heat until crisp, about 5 minutes, then let it cool slightly.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the cooked lentils, bell pepper, red onion, and parsley.
  3. Add the browned chorizo and toss with the olive oil and vinegar dressing.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste and serve at room temperature.
