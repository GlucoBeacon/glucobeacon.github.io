Light Indio Viejo

55 min total · 4 servings

A real Nicaraguan shredded beef and cornmeal stew with mint and orange -- traditional indio viejo comfort, made with a lighter hand on the corn masa and extra vegetables.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Traditional indio viejo uses a generous amount of corn masa to thicken the stew, which raises its glycemic load. Using a smaller amount of masa and bulking the stew up with extra vegetables and the real shredded beef keeps the dish's distinctive mint-and-citrus flavor while lowering how quickly it raises blood sugar.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb675 g beef, cooked and shredded
  • 1/3 cup45 g fine cornmeal, dissolved in a little broth
  • 11 onion, chopped
  • 11 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 22 tomatoes, diced
  • 2 tbsp30 ml fresh sour orange or lime juice
  • 2 tbsp8 g fresh mint, chopped
  • 4 cups950 ml low-sodium beef broth

Instructions

  1. Sauté the onion and bell pepper in a large pot until soft, about 6 minutes.
  2. Add the tomato and cook 3 minutes more.
  3. Add the shredded beef and broth, and bring to a simmer.
  4. Stir in the dissolved cornmeal and simmer 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the stew thickens.
  5. Stir in the sour orange juice and fresh mint just before serving, and season with salt to taste.
