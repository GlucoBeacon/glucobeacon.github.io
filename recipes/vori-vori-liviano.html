Light Vori Vori Soup

55 min total · 4 servings

Paraguay's real chicken soup studded with small cornmeal-and-cheese dumplings -- true vori vori comfort, with smaller dumplings and a vegetable-packed broth.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

The little cornmeal dumplings are the fastest-digesting part of this soup. Making them smaller and packing the broth with extra carrot, celery, and squash means the dumplings still deliver their real vori vori texture and flavor, but they make up a smaller share of the total meal, which softens the overall effect on blood sugar.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb675 g bone-in chicken thighs, skin removed
  • 1/2 cup70 g fine cornmeal
  • 1/4 cup30 g grated cheese
  • 11 egg
  • 22 carrots, diced
  • 22 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 cup150 g pumpkin or squash, diced
  • 7 cups1.7 L low-sodium chicken broth

Instructions

  1. Simmer the chicken thighs in the broth until cooked through, about 20 minutes. Remove, shred, and set aside.
  2. Mix the cornmeal, grated cheese, and egg into a stiff dough, and roll into small marble-sized balls.
  3. Add the carrot, celery, and squash to the simmering broth and cook 10 minutes.
  4. Drop the small dumplings into the simmering broth and cook 8-10 minutes until they float and are cooked through.
  5. Return the shredded chicken to the pot, season with salt and pepper, and serve hot.
