Tofu and Broccoli Stir-Fry

30 min total · 2 servings

Crispy pan-fried tofu tossed with broccoli and bell pepper in a garlic-soy sauce.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

With only 15g of carbs per serving, it's a lighter-carb option without needing a strict low-carb framing, and its estimated glycemic index of ~40 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 11g of protein per serving also helps slow digestion and blunt any post-meal rise.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cup355 ml tofu
  • 1 1/2 cup355 ml broccoli
  • 1/2 cup120 ml bell pepper
  • 1/2 cup120 ml carrots
  • 2 clove2 clove garlic
  • 1 tsp5 ml ginger
  • 2 tbsp30 ml low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tsp5 ml sesame oil

Instructions

  1. Press and cube tofu; pan-fry until golden on all sides.
  2. Remove tofu; stir-fry broccoli, pepper, and carrots 4 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and ginger; cook 1 minute.
  4. Whisk soy sauce, sesame oil, and cornstarch with a splash of water; add to pan.
  5. Return tofu and toss until sauce thickens.
