Oven-Baked Chicken Milanesa with Salad

40 min total · 4 servings

Argentina's everyday breaded chicken cutlet, baked instead of fried and served with a fresh salad instead of fries -- real weeknight milanesa, lighter without losing the crunch.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Frying adds absorbed oil and often pairs with fried potatoes, both of which push up calories and glycemic load. Baking the breaded cutlet with a light coating and serving it with salad instead of fries keeps the same familiar crunch and flavor while cutting the added fat and carbs significantly.

Ingredients

  • 44 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, pounded thin
  • 1 cup100 g whole wheat breadcrumbs
  • 22 eggs, beaten
  • 1 tsp1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tbsp15 ml olive oil, for the pan
  • 8 cups8 cups mixed salad greens with tomato, for serving

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and lightly oil a baking sheet.
  2. Mix the breadcrumbs with oregano, salt, and pepper.
  3. Dip each chicken breast in beaten egg, then coat in the breadcrumb mixture, pressing gently to adhere.
  4. Arrange on the baking sheet and bake 20-25 minutes, flipping once, until golden and cooked through.
  5. Serve with a large fresh salad in place of the traditional fries.
