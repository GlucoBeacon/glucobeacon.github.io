Kadhi

30 min total · 4 servings

A real North Indian tangy yogurt and gram-flour curry, tempered with mustard seeds and curry leaves -- comforting, protein-rich, and naturally gluten-free.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

With only 12g of carbs per serving, it's a lighter-carb option without needing a strict low-carb framing, and its estimated glycemic index of ~30 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast.

Instructions

  1. Whisk the yogurt and gram flour together with the water until smooth and lump-free.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot and add the mustard seeds, cumin seeds, and curry leaves until they sizzle.
  3. Add the ginger and green chili, and sauté 1 minute.
  4. Pour in the yogurt mixture along with the turmeric, and bring to a gentle simmer, stirring frequently to prevent curdling.
  5. Simmer 12-15 minutes until slightly thickened, season with salt, and serve hot.
