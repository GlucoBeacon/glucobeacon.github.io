Tortilla Española

35 min total · 4 servings

The classic Spanish potato and egg omelette, real-adapted for a lower glycemic load -- half the usual potato, extra egg, and cauliflower filling in the gap, so it keeps the real texture without the real spike.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Cutting the potato in half and filling the volume with cauliflower keeps the dish's real texture while meaningfully lowering both total carbs and glycemic load compared to a traditional all-potato version -- the extra egg adds protein that further slows how fast the remaining carbs hit your bloodstream.

Ingredients

  • 6 large6 large eggs
  • 1 medium1 medium potato, thinly sliced
  • 1.5 cups150 g cauliflower florets, thinly sliced
  • 1 small1 small onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp45 ml olive oil

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a non-stick pan, gently cook the potato, cauliflower, and onion slices over low heat until tender (not browned), about 12 minutes.
  2. Drain excess oil and let the vegetables cool slightly.
  3. Whisk the eggs with salt, then fold in the potato-cauliflower-onion mixture.
  4. Pour back into the pan, cook over medium-low heat 5 minutes until the bottom sets.
  5. Flip (using a plate) and cook the other side 3-4 minutes until just set in the center.
