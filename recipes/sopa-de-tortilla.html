Sopa de Tortilla

35 min total · 3 servings

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

This soup is mostly broth and vegetables, so the carb load per bowl is modest, and the fat from avocado and cheese helps slow digestion of what carbs are there.

Instructions

  1. Roast the tomatoes, then blend with the pasilla chile and garlic until smooth.
  2. Sauté the diced onion in a bit of oil until soft, add the blended tomato mixture, and cook 5 minutes.
  3. Add the broth, simmer 15 minutes, and season with salt.
  4. Bake the tortilla strips at 400°F (200°C) for 8 minutes until crisp.
  5. Ladle the soup into bowls and top with tortilla strips, avocado, and queso fresco.
