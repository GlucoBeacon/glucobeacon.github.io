Roasted Garlic Cauliflower

30 min total · 2 servings

Golden roasted cauliflower with garlic and lemon — a versatile low-carb side.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 8g of carbs per serving, it fits comfortably within most low-carb diabetes meal plans, and its estimated glycemic index of ~15 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast.

Ingredients

  • 2 cup475 ml cauliflower
  • 3 clove3 clove garlic
  • 1 tbsp15 ml olive oil
  • 1 tbsp15 ml lemon juice
  • 1/4 tsp1 ml black pepper
  • 1 tbsp15 ml fresh parsley

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Toss cauliflower florets with olive oil, minced garlic, and pepper.
  3. Roast 20–25 minutes until golden.
  4. Drizzle with lemon juice and garnish with parsley.
