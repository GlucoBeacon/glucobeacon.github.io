Mushroom Risotto

45 min total · 2 servings

Creamy arborio rice slowly simmered with mushrooms, parmesan, and white wine.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 67g of carbs per serving, it's worth pairing with protein or fat and watching portion size to keep the resulting blood sugar rise gradual, and its estimated glycemic index of ~55 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 2g of fiber per serving adds the same slowing effect.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup175 ml arborio rice
  • 1 1/2 cup355 ml mushrooms
  • 3 cup710 ml low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 1/4 cup60 ml white wine
  • 1/4 cup60 ml onion
  • 2 clove2 clove garlic
  • 3 tbsp45 ml parmesan cheese
  • 1 tbsp15 ml butter
  • 1 tbsp15 ml olive oil

Instructions

  1. Sauté mushrooms in olive oil until golden; set aside.
  2. Sauté onion and garlic in the same pan.
  3. Add rice and toast 1 minute; deglaze with white wine.
  4. Add warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring until absorbed, about 25 minutes.
  5. Stir in mushrooms, parmesan, and butter before serving.
