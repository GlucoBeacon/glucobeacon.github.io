Light Bolivian Peanut Soup

50 min total · 4 servings

Bolivia's beloved creamy peanut soup with beef and vegetables -- real sopa de maní comfort, served without the traditional fried potato straw topping.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Peanuts themselves have a low glycemic index and are rich in protein and healthy fat, so the soup's base is naturally gentle on blood sugar. The traditional crispy fried potato straws on top add a significant dose of fast-digesting starch and oil; leaving them off keeps the real peanut-and-vegetable flavor while removing the biggest glycemic spike in the dish.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb225 g lean beef, cubed
  • 1/2 cup125 g natural peanut butter (no added sugar)
  • 11 onion, chopped
  • 11 carrot, diced
  • 1 cup100 g green peas
  • 11 small potato, cubed
  • 6 cups1.4 L low-sodium beef broth

Instructions

  1. Brown the beef with the onion in a large pot, about 8 minutes.
  2. Add the broth and simmer 20 minutes until the beef is tender.
  3. Whisk in the peanut butter until fully dissolved into the broth.
  4. Add the carrot, potato, and peas, and simmer 15 more minutes until tender.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste and serve hot, without a fried garnish.
