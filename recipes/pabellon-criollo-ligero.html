Light Pabellón Criollo

70 min total · 4 servings

Venezuela's national dish -- shredded beef, black beans, and plantain -- rebalanced with a smaller plantain portion and a fresh side salad in place of white rice.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

The traditional plate stacks white rice and sweet fried plantain together, both fast-digesting carbs. Keeping the real shredded beef and black beans -- the dish's flavorful, fiber-rich core -- while replacing the rice with a fresh salad and using just a small amount of plantain keeps the meal authentic while lowering its overall glycemic load.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb680 g flank steak, cooked and shredded
  • 11 onion, sliced
  • 11 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 1/2 cups430 g cooked black beans
  • 3/43/4 small ripe plantain, sliced and baked (not fried)
  • 8 cups290 g mixed salad greens, for serving
  • 2 tbsp25 ml olive oil

Instructions

  1. Sauté the onion and bell pepper in olive oil until soft, about 6 minutes.
  2. Add the shredded beef and cook 5 more minutes to heat through and combine flavors.
  3. Warm the black beans separately in a small pot.
  4. Bake the plantain slices at 400°F (200°C) for 15 minutes until golden, instead of frying.
  5. Plate the beef, black beans, and a few plantain slices alongside a large portion of mixed salad greens instead of rice.
