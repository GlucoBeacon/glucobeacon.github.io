Venezuelan Pumpkin Coconut Soup

35 min total · 4 servings

A creamy pumpkin soup with a hint of coconut milk -- real Venezuelan auyama comfort, portioned to keep it light.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Pumpkin has a moderate glycemic index, but pureeing it with its natural fiber intact and keeping the portion modest -- rather than pairing it with arepas or bread -- means the carbs digest more gradually, while the coconut milk adds real richness without added sugar.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups600 g auyama (pumpkin), cubed
  • 11 onion, chopped
  • 1/4 cup60 ml light coconut milk
  • 4 cups950 ml low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 1 tbsp15 ml olive oil

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a pot and sauté the onion until soft, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the pumpkin and broth, bring to a boil, then simmer 20 minutes until the pumpkin is very tender.
  3. Puree with an immersion blender until smooth, then stir in the coconut milk.
  4. Season with salt and pepper and serve hot.
