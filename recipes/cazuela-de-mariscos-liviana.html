Light Seafood Cazuela

40 min total · 4 servings

A real Venezuelan coastal seafood stew with tomato, bell pepper, and coconut milk -- naturally low in carbs and rich in lean protein.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

This stew is built almost entirely from shrimp, fish, tomato, and bell pepper -- there's no rice or starch mixed in, so its carbohydrate content stays naturally low while it delivers a large, satisfying portion of protein.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb225 g shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1/2 lb225 g firm white fish fillet, cubed
  • 11 onion, chopped
  • 11 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 22 tomatoes, diced
  • 3 cloves3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup120 ml light coconut milk
  • 1/4 cup10 g fresh cilantro, chopped

Instructions

  1. Sauté the onion, bell pepper, and garlic in a large pot until soft, about 6 minutes.
  2. Add the tomato and cook 3 minutes more.
  3. Stir in the coconut milk and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Add the fish and shrimp, and simmer 6-8 minutes until just cooked through.
  5. Season with salt and pepper, stir in fresh cilantro, and serve hot.
