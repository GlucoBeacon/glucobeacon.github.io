Light Grilled Fish, Tipitapa-Style

35 min total · 4 servings

A real Nicaraguan whole-fish dish inspired by the fried fish of Tipitapa, made grilled instead of deep-fried and topped with fresh cabbage and onion.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

The classic Tipitapa-style fish is deep-fried whole, which adds a substantial amount of absorbed oil without adding any real carbs. Grilling the same fish instead removes that added fat while keeping the tangy cabbage-and-onion topping that gives the dish its real character, making this version lighter overall.

Ingredients

  • 44 whole small fish or thick fish fillets
  • 2 tbsp30 ml fresh lime juice
  • 2 tbsp30 ml olive oil
  • 2 cups150 g cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1/21/2 onion, thinly sliced
  • 11 tomato, diced

Instructions

  1. Season the fish with salt, pepper, and half the lime juice.
  2. Grill over medium-high heat, about 5-6 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through.
  3. Toss the cabbage, onion, and tomato with the olive oil and remaining lime juice.
  4. Serve the grilled fish topped with the fresh cabbage slaw.
