Bhindi Masala

30 min total · 4 servings

A real North Indian dry-spiced okra stir-fry with onion and tomato -- naturally low in carbs, quick to make, and a classic everyday side.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Instructions

  1. Wash and thoroughly dry the okra, then trim and slice into rounds.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and sauté the onion until soft, about 4 minutes.
  3. Add the okra, turmeric, coriander powder, and red chili powder, and cook 10-12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender and lightly browned.
  4. Add the tomato and salt, and cook 4-5 more minutes.
  5. Serve hot.
