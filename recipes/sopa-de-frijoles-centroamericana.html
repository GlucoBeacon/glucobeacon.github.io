Central American Bean Soup

45 min total · 4 servings

A real, comforting Central American black bean soup with onion, cumin, and a touch of sour cream -- everyday home cooking, naturally slow-digesting.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Black beans are naturally low-GI and rich in soluble fiber, so this soup digests gradually rather than in a quick spike. Relying entirely on the beans as the carb source, with just a small dollop of sour cream for richness, keeps this a genuinely gentle, filling meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups400 g dried black beans, soaked overnight
  • 11 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp1 tsp ground cumin
  • 6 cups1.4 L low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 1/4 cup60 g light sour cream, for serving

Instructions

  1. Drain the soaked beans and add them to a large pot with the broth, onion, garlic, and cumin.
  2. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 30-35 minutes until the beans are very tender.
  3. Puree half the soup with an immersion blender for a thicker texture, leaving the rest chunky.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste, and serve hot with a small dollop of light sour cream.
