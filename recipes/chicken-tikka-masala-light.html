Chicken Tikka Masala, Light

60 min total · 4 servings

A real North Indian creamy tomato-based chicken curry, made with a lighter hand on cream -- true tikka masala comfort, naturally low in carbs.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 10g of carbs per serving, it fits comfortably within most low-carb diabetes meal plans, and its estimated glycemic index of ~25 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 33g of protein per serving also helps slow digestion and blunt any post-meal rise.

Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken cubes in half the yogurt, half the garlic and ginger, and half the garam masala for at least 20 minutes.
  2. Heat a little oil in a pan and sear the marinated chicken until lightly charred and cooked through, about 8 minutes. Set aside.
  3. In the same pan, heat the remaining oil and sauté the onion, remaining garlic, and ginger until golden, about 6 minutes.
  4. Add the tomato puree, remaining garam masala, red chili powder, and turmeric, and simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Stir in the remaining yogurt and the cream, add the seared chicken back in, and simmer 5 minutes. Season with salt and serve hot.
