Light Gallo Pinto

25 min total · 4 servings

Nicaragua's everyday rice-and-beans breakfast -- real gallo pinto flavor, rebalanced toward more beans and less rice.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Traditional gallo pinto is roughly equal parts rice and beans, but rice digests much faster than beans and drives most of the dish's glycemic impact. Shifting the ratio toward more beans -- naturally low-GI and high in fiber -- while using less rice keeps the same real, oniony gallo pinto flavor with a gentler effect on blood sugar.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups500 g cooked red or black beans, with a little of their liquid
  • 1 cup185 g cooked white rice
  • 11 onion, finely chopped
  • 11 green bell pepper, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp30 ml vegetable oil
  • 11 avocado, sliced, for serving

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large skillet and sauté the onion and bell pepper until soft, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the beans with their liquid and mash a small portion of them against the pan to thicken.
  3. Stir in the rice and cook 5-7 minutes, stirring frequently, until everything is well combined and heated through.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste and serve with avocado slices on the side.
