Sinigang na Baboy

60 min total · 4 servings

A sour tamarind-broth pork and vegetable stew -- real Filipino comfort food, tangy and light despite being hearty. Nutrition is for the stew alone; rice on the side counts separately toward your carb budget.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

This dish is almost entirely broth, pork, and vegetables with very little starch of its own, so the carb count stays low -- just be mindful of how much rice you pair it with.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb680 g pork ribs or pork belly, cubed
  • 6 cups1.4 L water
  • 2 tbsp30 ml tamarind soup base (sinigang mix) or fresh tamarind pulp
  • 1 medium1 medium radish, sliced
  • 2 cups60 g kangkong (water spinach) or spinach
  • 1 cup100 g string beans, cut
  • 2 medium2 medium tomatoes, quartered
  • 11 onion, quartered
  • 22 green chilies, whole

Instructions

  1. Simmer the pork in water with the onion and tomatoes until tender, about 30 minutes, skimming off any foam.
  2. Stir in the tamarind soup base until dissolved.
  3. Add the radish and string beans, simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Add the kangkong/spinach and green chilies, cook 2-3 minutes until just wilted.
  5. Season with fish sauce to taste and serve hot.
