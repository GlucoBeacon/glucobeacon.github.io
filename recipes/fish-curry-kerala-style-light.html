Fish Curry, Kerala-Style Light

40 min total · 4 servings

A real South Indian coconut milk fish curry from Kerala, with mustard seeds, curry leaves, and tamarind -- true coastal comfort, made with a lighter hand on coconut milk.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 8g of carbs per serving, it fits comfortably within most low-carb diabetes meal plans, and its estimated glycemic index of ~20 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 28g of protein per serving also helps slow digestion and blunt any post-meal rise.

Instructions

  1. Season the fish with salt and turmeric, and set aside.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and add the mustard seeds and curry leaves until they sizzle.
  3. Add the onion, garlic, and ginger, and sauté until soft, about 5 minutes.
  4. Add the tomato, red chili powder, and tamarind paste, and cook 5 minutes until the tomato breaks down.
  5. Stir in the coconut milk and bring to a gentle simmer, then add the fish and simmer 8-10 minutes until just cooked through. Serve hot.
