Peruvian Fish Ceviche

25 min total · 4 servings

Peru's national dish: fresh white fish 'cooked' in lime juice with red onion, cilantro, and chili -- real ceviche, naturally low-carb and light.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Ceviche is built almost entirely from lean fish, citrus, and vegetables -- there's no starch or added sugar in the dish at all, so it has close to zero glycemic impact while still delivering a full serving of high-quality protein.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb450 g firm white fish fillet (such as sea bass or tilapia), cubed
  • 1 cup240 ml fresh lime juice
  • 11 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 11 aji amarillo or jalapeño chili, minced
  • 1/4 cup10 g fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 11 sweet potato, boiled and sliced, for serving (optional, small portion)

Instructions

  1. Place the cubed fish in a glass or ceramic bowl and cover with the lime juice.
  2. Add the red onion, chili, and a pinch of salt, and toss gently.
  3. Let it marinate ('cook' in the citrus) in the refrigerator for 10-15 minutes, until the fish turns opaque.
  4. Stir in the cilantro right before serving.
  5. Serve chilled, with a small slice of boiled sweet potato on the side if desired.
