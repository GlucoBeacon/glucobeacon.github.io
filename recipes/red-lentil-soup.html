Red Lentil Soup

35 min total · 2 servings

Warming red lentil soup with cumin and turmeric — rich in fiber and plant protein.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 40g of carbs per serving, it's worth pairing with protein or fat and watching portion size to keep the resulting blood sugar rise gradual, and its estimated glycemic index of ~30 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 7g of fiber per serving adds the same slowing effect.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup120 ml red lentils
  • 2 cup475 ml low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 1 medium1 medium carrots
  • 1 stalk1 stalk celery
  • 1/2 medium1/2 medium onion
  • 2 clove2 clove garlic
  • 1 tsp5 ml cumin
  • 1/2 tsp2 ml turmeric
  • 1 tsp5 ml olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp1 ml black pepper

Instructions

  1. Sauté onion, carrot, and celery in olive oil for 5 minutes.
  2. Add garlic, cumin, and turmeric; cook 1 minute.
  3. Add lentils and broth; bring to a boil.
  4. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer 20 minutes until lentils are soft.
  5. Season with black pepper and serve.
