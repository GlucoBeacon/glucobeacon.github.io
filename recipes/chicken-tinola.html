Chicken Tinola

40 min total · 4 servings

A gentle ginger-chicken soup with green papaya and chili leaves -- real Filipino home cooking, light and warming.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

This is a broth-based soup with lean chicken thigh and fibrous green papaya -- very low in carbs and starch, so it fits almost any diabetes meal plan without much adjustment.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb680 g chicken thighs, bone-in, skin removed
  • 2 tbsp20 g ginger, sliced
  • 11 onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 6 cups1.4 L water or chicken broth
  • 2 cups300 g green papaya, sliced (or chayote)
  • 2 cups60 g spinach or moringa (malunggay) leaves

Instructions

  1. Sauté the ginger, onion, and garlic in a bit of oil until fragrant.
  2. Add the chicken and sear a few minutes.
  3. Pour in the water/broth and simmer 20 minutes until the chicken is tender.
  4. Add the green papaya, simmer 8 minutes until slightly soft.
  5. Stir in the spinach/moringa leaves, cook 2 minutes, and season with fish sauce.
