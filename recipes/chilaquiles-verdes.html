Chilaquiles Verdes

25 min total · 2 servings

Crispy tortilla chips simmered in tangy green salsa, topped with a fried egg and queso fresco -- a real Mexican breakfast classic, baked instead of fried to keep it lighter.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Corn tortillas have a moderate glycemic index on their own, but baking them instead of deep-frying and pairing them with egg and cheese slows digestion and blunts the blood sugar response.

Ingredients

  • 55 corn tortillas, cut into triangles
  • 7/8 cup200 ml tomatillo salsa verde
  • 2 large2 large eggs
  • 3 tbsp30 g queso fresco, crumbled
  • 2 tbsp25 ml crema or sour cream
  • 1/4 cup40 g white onion, diced

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Arrange tortilla triangles on a baking sheet and bake 8-10 minutes until crisp.
  2. Warm the salsa verde in a pan over medium heat for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the baked tortilla chips to the salsa and toss gently until just coated -- they should stay slightly crisp, not soggy.
  4. In a separate pan, fry the eggs to your liking.
  5. Plate the chilaquiles, top with a fried egg, queso fresco, crema, and diced onion.
