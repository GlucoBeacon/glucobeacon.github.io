Paraguayan Lentil Stew

45 min total · 4 servings

A real Paraguayan home-style lentil stew with tomato, bell pepper, and cumin -- everyday comfort food, naturally slow-digesting and high in fiber.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Lentils are naturally low-GI and rich in soluble fiber, so this everyday stew digests gradually rather than spiking blood sugar. Relying on the lentils themselves as the main carb source, without adding rice, keeps this a genuinely gentle, filling meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups400 g dried lentils, rinsed
  • 11 onion, chopped
  • 11 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 22 tomatoes, diced
  • 3 cloves3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp1 tsp ground cumin
  • 5 cups1.2 L low-sodium vegetable broth

Instructions

  1. Sauté the onion, bell pepper, and garlic in a large pot until soft, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the tomato and cumin, and cook 2 minutes more.
  3. Add the lentils and broth, and bring to a boil.
  4. Reduce heat and simmer 30 minutes, until the lentils are tender and the stew has thickened.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste and serve hot.
