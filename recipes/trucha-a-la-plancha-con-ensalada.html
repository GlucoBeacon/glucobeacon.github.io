Grilled Trout with Salad

22 min total · 4 servings

Fresh Lake Titicaca-style trout, pan-seared with garlic and lime -- real Bolivian highland cooking, light and quick.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Trout is a lean, omega-3-rich fish with essentially no carbohydrates, so a simple pan-seared fillet has almost no effect on blood sugar. Pairing it with a fresh salad instead of rice or fried potato keeps the whole plate close to zero glycemic impact.

Ingredients

  • 44 trout fillets
  • 3 cloves3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tbsp45 ml fresh lime juice
  • 2 tbsp30 ml olive oil
  • 6 cups6 cups mixed salad greens
  • 11 tomato, sliced

Instructions

  1. Marinate the trout fillets in garlic, half the lime juice, salt, and pepper for 10 minutes.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat and sear the trout skin-side down first, 3-4 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through.
  3. Toss the salad greens and tomato with the remaining lime juice.
  4. Serve the trout hot alongside the fresh salad.
