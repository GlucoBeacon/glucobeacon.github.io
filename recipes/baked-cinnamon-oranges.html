Baked Cinnamon Oranges

10 min total · 2 servings

Broiled orange slices dusted with cinnamon and topped with pistachios and a light honey drizzle.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 22g of carbs per serving, it fits comfortably within most low-carb diabetes meal plans, and its estimated glycemic index of ~40 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast.

Ingredients

  • 2 whole orange, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp pistachios, chopped
  • 1 tsp honey

Instructions

  1. Arrange the orange slices on a baking sheet and dust with cinnamon.
  2. Broil for 4–5 minutes until lightly caramelized at the edges.
  3. Top with chopped pistachios and a light drizzle of honey.
