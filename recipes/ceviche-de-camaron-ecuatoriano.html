Ecuadorian Shrimp Ceviche

30 min total · 4 servings

Ecuador's tangy tomato-based shrimp ceviche, distinct from its Peruvian cousin -- real coastal flavor, naturally light and protein-rich.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

This ceviche is built from lean shrimp, tomato juice, lime, and onion -- there's no rice or fried garnish weighing it down, so the carbs stay low and the meal digests gently while still delivering a big serving of protein.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb450 g shrimp, peeled, deveined, and cooked
  • 1 cup240 ml fresh tomato juice
  • 1/2 cup120 ml fresh lime juice
  • 11 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 11 tomato, diced
  • 1/4 cup10 g fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 11 avocado, sliced, for serving

Instructions

  1. Combine the tomato juice, lime juice, red onion, and diced tomato in a large bowl.
  2. Add the cooked shrimp and toss to coat evenly.
  3. Refrigerate 15-20 minutes so the flavors meld.
  4. Stir in fresh cilantro and season with salt to taste.
  5. Serve chilled with avocado slices on top.
