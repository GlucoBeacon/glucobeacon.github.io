Light Peruvian Creole Noodle Soup

40 min total · 4 servings

A comforting beef and noodle soup with a hint of milk and oregano -- real sopa criolla warmth, made with whole wheat noodles and extra vegetables to keep it gentler on blood sugar.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Sopa a la criolla traditionally uses a generous portion of regular wheat noodles, which digest fast in a hot broth. Swapping in whole wheat noodles, using a smaller noodle portion, and bulking the bowl up with extra vegetables slows digestion and adds fiber, while the milk and oregano keep the same comforting, familiar flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb225 g lean ground beef
  • 6 oz170 g whole wheat spaghetti, broken into pieces
  • 11 onion, chopped
  • 22 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 cup150 g mixed vegetables (carrots, peas), diced
  • 1/2 cup120 ml low-fat milk
  • 11 egg, poached, for topping (optional)
  • 1 tsp1 tsp dried oregano
  • 5 cups1.2 L low-sodium beef broth

Instructions

  1. Brown the ground beef with the onion in a large pot over medium heat, about 6 minutes.
  2. Add the tomatoes, oregano, and broth, and bring to a boil.
  3. Add the mixed vegetables and broken spaghetti, and simmer 12-15 minutes until the pasta is tender.
  4. Stir in the milk and heat through without boiling.
  5. Ladle into bowls and top each with a poached egg if desired.
