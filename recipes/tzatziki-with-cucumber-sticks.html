Tzatziki with Cucumber Sticks

10 min total · 4 servings

Cool Greek yogurt dip with grated cucumber, garlic, and dill, served with fresh cucumber sticks.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 7g of carbs per serving, it fits comfortably within most low-carb diabetes meal plans, and its estimated glycemic index of ~15 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cup355 ml plain greek yogurt
  • 1 large1 large cucumber
  • 1 clove1 clove garlic
  • 1 tbsp15 ml fresh dill
  • 1 tbsp15 ml lemon juice
  • 1 tsp5 ml olive oil

Instructions

  1. Squeeze excess water from the grated cucumber.
  2. Mix the yogurt, cucumber, garlic, dill, lemon juice, and olive oil.
  3. Chill for at least 15 minutes and serve with cucumber sticks.
