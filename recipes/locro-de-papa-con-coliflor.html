Potato and Cauliflower Cheese Soup

45 min total · 4 servings

A lighter take on Ecuador's beloved potato-and-cheese soup -- real locro warmth and avocado garnish, with most of the potato swapped for cauliflower.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Traditional locro is made almost entirely from potato, which is high-GI and easy to overeat in a creamy, cheesy soup. Replacing most of the potato with cauliflower -- keeping just enough for authentic texture and flavor -- cuts the carb load significantly while keeping the same comforting, cheesy soup everyone recognizes.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups500 g cauliflower florets
  • 11 small potato, cubed
  • 11 onion, chopped
  • 1 tsp1 tsp ground achiote or paprika
  • 1/2 cup60 g shredded low-fat mozzarella or queso fresco
  • 4 cups950 ml low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 11 avocado, sliced, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Sauté the onion with achiote or paprika in a pot until soft, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the cauliflower, potato, and broth, and simmer 20 minutes until very tender.
  3. Mash roughly with a potato masher, leaving some texture rather than a smooth puree.
  4. Stir in the shredded cheese until melted.
  5. Season with salt to taste, and serve topped with avocado slices.
