Sprouted Moong Salad

10 min total · 4 servings

A real North Indian raw sprouted mung bean salad with cucumber, tomato, and lime -- fresh, crunchy, and naturally high in plant protein and fiber.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

With only 15g of carbs per serving, it's a lighter-carb option without needing a strict low-carb framing, and its estimated glycemic index of ~25 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 7g of protein per serving also helps slow digestion and blunt any post-meal rise, and 5g of fiber per serving adds the same slowing effect.

Instructions

  1. Combine the sprouted moong beans, cucumber, tomato, and red onion in a large bowl.
  2. Drizzle with the lime juice and toss well.
  3. Season with salt and black pepper.
  4. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve chilled or at room temperature.
