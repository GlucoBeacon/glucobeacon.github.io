Light Chicken and Corn Ajiaco

60 min total · 4 servings

Bogotá's beloved chicken and potato soup with corn and the traditional guascas herb -- real ajiaco comfort, rebalanced with more chicken and vegetables and a smaller potato portion.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Restaurant ajiaco often uses three types of potato in a generous portion, which adds up to a high glycemic load. This version keeps the real guascas-and-corn flavor but cuts the potato back to one modest portion and adds extra shredded chicken and green beans, so the bowl stays filling and authentic with less impact on blood sugar.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb450 g boneless chicken breast
  • 11 medium potato, cubed
  • 1 cup150 g corn on the cob, cut into rounds
  • 1 cup100 g green beans, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp4 g dried guascas herb (or substitute dried oregano)
  • 6 cups1.4 L low-sodium chicken broth
  • 11 avocado, sliced, for serving

Instructions

  1. Simmer the chicken breast in the broth with the guascas until cooked through, about 20 minutes. Remove, shred, and set aside.
  2. Add the potato to the same broth and simmer 15 minutes.
  3. Add the corn and green beans, and simmer 10 more minutes until everything is tender.
  4. Return the shredded chicken to the pot and heat through.
  5. Season with salt and pepper, and serve with avocado slices on the side.
