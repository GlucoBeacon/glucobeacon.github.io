Light Seco de Pollo

50 min total · 4 servings

Ecuador's classic cilantro-braised chicken stew -- real seco flavor, served with extra vegetables instead of the traditional heaping side of rice.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Seco de pollo itself -- chicken braised in a cilantro, garlic, and beer sauce -- has very little carbohydrate on its own. Restaurant versions typically pile a large mound of white rice next to it; skipping the rice and serving it with roasted or sautéed vegetables instead keeps the real braised-chicken flavor while dramatically lowering the meal's glycemic load.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb675 g boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 cup40 g fresh cilantro, blended into a paste with a little water
  • 11 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup240 ml light beer or low-sodium chicken broth
  • 3 cups3 cups mixed roasted vegetables (zucchini, bell pepper, onion), for serving

Instructions

  1. Sauté the onion and garlic in a large pot until soft, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the chicken thighs and brown lightly on both sides.
  3. Stir in the cilantro paste and beer or broth, cover, and simmer 25-30 minutes until the chicken is tender.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Serve hot alongside a generous portion of roasted mixed vegetables instead of rice.
