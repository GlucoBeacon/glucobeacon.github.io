Greek Pasta Salad

25 min total · 2 servings

Cold pasta salad with feta, cucumber, olives, and a bright lemon-herb dressing.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 62g of carbs per serving, it's worth pairing with protein or fat and watching portion size to keep the resulting blood sugar rise gradual, and its estimated glycemic index of ~50 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 4g of fiber per serving adds the same slowing effect.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cup355 ml pasta
  • 1/3 cup80 ml low-sodium feta cheese
  • 1/2 cup120 ml cucumber
  • 1/2 cup120 ml cherry tomatoes
  • 8 piece8 piece kalamata olives
  • 2 tbsp30 ml red onion
  • 2 tbsp30 ml olive oil
  • 1 tbsp15 ml red wine vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp2 ml dried oregano

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta according to package directions; cool.
  2. Combine pasta with feta, cucumber, tomatoes, olives, and red onion.
  3. Whisk olive oil, vinegar, and oregano; toss with salad.
