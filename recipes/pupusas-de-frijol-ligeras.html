Light Bean Pupusas

45 min total · 4 servings

El Salvador's beloved stuffed corn cakes -- real pupusa flavor, made smaller with a higher bean-to-masa ratio and served with tangy curtido cabbage slaw.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Pupusas are traditionally made almost entirely from corn masa, which digests quickly. Making smaller pupusas with a generous bean filling shifts the ratio toward the beans -- naturally low-GI and high in fiber -- so the same beloved flavor comes with a gentler rise in blood sugar, especially paired with the tangy, low-carb curtido on top.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups180 g masa harina (corn flour for tortillas)
  • 2 cups350 g cooked and mashed refried beans
  • 1/2 cup60 g shredded low-fat mozzarella
  • 2 cups150 g cabbage, finely shredded, for curtido
  • 1/4 cup60 ml white vinegar
  • 11 carrot, shredded, for curtido

Instructions

  1. Toss the cabbage and carrot with the vinegar and a pinch of salt, and let sit at least 20 minutes to make the curtido.
  2. Mix the masa harina with warm water until it forms a soft, pliable dough.
  3. Combine the mashed beans and cheese for the filling.
  4. Form small balls of dough, flatten into a disc, place a spoonful of filling in the center, and fold and pat back into a small flat cake.
  5. Cook on a hot, lightly oiled griddle 3-4 minutes per side until golden with brown spots, and serve with the curtido.
