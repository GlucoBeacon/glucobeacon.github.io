Fig & Almond Overnight Oats

10 min total · 2 servings

Rolled oats soaked overnight with Greek yogurt, chopped dried figs, and sliced almonds.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 43g of carbs per serving, it's worth pairing with protein or fat and watching portion size to keep the resulting blood sugar rise gradual, and its estimated glycemic index of ~45 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 7g of fiber per serving adds the same slowing effect.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup plain greek yogurt
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 3 whole dried figs
  • 2 tbsp almonds
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Combine the oats, yogurt, milk, figs, almonds, and cinnamon in a jar.
  2. Refrigerate overnight.
  3. Stir and serve cold, adding a splash of milk if needed.
