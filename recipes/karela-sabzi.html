Diabetic-Friendly Dinner

Karela Sabzi

40 min total · 4 servings

A real North Indian dry-spiced bitter gourd stir-fry with onion -- an everyday side prized for its naturally low glycemic impact.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 9g of carbs per serving, it fits comfortably within most low-carb diabetes meal plans, and its estimated glycemic index of ~15 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast.

Instructions

  1. Slice the bitter gourd thinly, rub with a little salt, and let sit 15 minutes to draw out some bitterness, then rinse and pat dry.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and sauté the onion until soft, about 4 minutes.
  3. Add the bitter gourd, turmeric, coriander powder, and red chili powder, and cook 12-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender and lightly browned.
  4. Season with salt to taste and serve hot.
