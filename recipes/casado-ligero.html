Light Casado Plate

45 min total · 4 servings

Costa Rica's everyday combo plate -- real casado flavor (grilled protein, beans, cabbage salad, plantain) with a smaller rice portion and extra vegetables.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

A traditional casado stacks white rice, beans, and sweet plantain together on one plate -- a lot of carbohydrate variety in one sitting. Cutting the rice portion back, keeping the beans (naturally low-GI) as the main starch, and adding extra cabbage salad keeps the real, balanced casado experience while lowering the plate's overall glycemic load.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb455 g chicken breast or lean beef, grilled
  • 1/2 cup80 g cooked white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups260 g cooked black beans
  • 4 cups355 g cabbage, shredded
  • 22 tomatoes, sliced
  • 3/43/4 small ripe plantain, sliced and baked
  • 2 tbsp25 ml olive oil and lime dressing

Instructions

  1. Season and grill the chicken or beef until cooked through, about 6-8 minutes per side.
  2. Warm the black beans in a small pot.
  3. Bake the plantain slices at 400°F (200°C) for 15 minutes until golden, instead of frying.
  4. Toss the cabbage and tomato with the olive oil and lime dressing.
  5. Plate the protein, rice, beans, plantain, and cabbage salad together as a balanced combo plate.
