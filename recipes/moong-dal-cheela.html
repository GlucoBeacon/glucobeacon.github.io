Moong Dal Cheela

40 min total · 4 servings

A real North Indian savory lentil crepe made from split yellow moong dal, onion, and warming spices -- naturally high in protein and fiber, and gently low-GI.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 22g of carbs per serving, it's worth pairing with protein or fat and watching portion size to keep the resulting blood sugar rise gradual, and its estimated glycemic index of ~35 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 11g of protein per serving also helps slow digestion and blunt any post-meal rise, and 6g of fiber per serving adds the same slowing effect.

Instructions

  1. Drain the soaked moong dal and blend with a little water until smooth, forming a thick, pourable batter.
  2. Stir in the onion, green chili, ginger, cumin seeds, turmeric, salt, and cilantro.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled nonstick griddle over medium heat and pour a ladleful of batter, spreading it into a thin round.
  4. Drizzle a few drops of oil around the edges and cook 2-3 minutes per side until golden and set.
  5. Serve hot, plain or with a side of mint chutney or yogurt.
