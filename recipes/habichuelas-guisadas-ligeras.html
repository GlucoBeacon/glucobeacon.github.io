Light Dominican Stewed Beans

45 min total · 4 servings

The Dominican Republic's everyday stewed red beans with sofrito and squash -- real habichuelas guisadas comfort, naturally slow-digesting.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Beans are naturally low-GI and rich in soluble fiber, so this real Dominican staple digests gradually. Relying on the beans themselves as the main carbohydrate source, without the traditional side of white rice, makes this a genuinely gentle option for blood sugar.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups500 g cooked red beans, with a little of their liquid
  • 11 onion, chopped
  • 11 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 3 cloves3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup150 g auyama (pumpkin), cubed
  • 1 tbsp30 g tomato paste
  • 3 cups700 ml low-sodium vegetable broth

Instructions

  1. Sauté the onion, bell pepper, and garlic in a large pot until soft, about 6 minutes.
  2. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 1-2 minutes.
  3. Add the beans, broth, and auyama, and bring to a boil.
  4. Simmer 20-25 minutes until the auyama is tender and the stew has thickened slightly.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste and serve hot.
