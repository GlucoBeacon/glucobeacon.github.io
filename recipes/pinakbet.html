Pinakbet

35 min total · 4 servings

A vegetable medley of squash, eggplant, okra, and bitter melon in a savory shrimp-paste sauce -- real Ilocano vegetable dish, full of fiber.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

This dish is almost entirely fibrous vegetables, which slows digestion and keeps the overall glycemic impact low even though it's a hearty, filling portion.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup120 g kalabasa (squash), cubed
  • 11 eggplant, sliced
  • 1 cup100 g okra
  • 1 cup100 g ampalaya (bitter melon), sliced
  • 1 cup100 g string beans, cut
  • 22 tomatoes, chopped
  • 11 onion, sliced
  • 1-2 tbsp15-30 ml bagoong (shrimp paste) or fish sauce

Instructions

  1. Sauté the garlic and onion in a bit of oil until fragrant.
  2. Add the tomatoes and cook until softened.
  3. Add the bagoong/fish sauce and a splash of water, bring to a simmer.
  4. Add the squash first, then the eggplant, string beans, okra, and bitter melon in stages so nothing overcooks.
  5. Simmer until the vegetables are tender but not mushy, about 10 minutes.
