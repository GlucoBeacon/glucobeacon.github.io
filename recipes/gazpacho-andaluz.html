Gazpacho Andaluz

75 min total · 4 servings

A chilled raw tomato and vegetable soup -- real Andalusian summer classic, refreshing and full of fiber. Mostly chilling time, minutes of real work.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

This soup is almost entirely raw vegetables with no added sugar or starch, so it's naturally low-carb and low-calorie while still being filling thanks to the fiber and healthy fat from olive oil.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb900 g ripe tomatoes, roughly chopped
  • 11 cucumber, peeled and chopped
  • 11 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 1/41/4 red onion
  • 1 clove1 clove garlic
  • 2 tbsp30 ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp15 ml sherry vinegar

Instructions

  1. Combine the tomatoes, cucumber, bell pepper, onion, and garlic in a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth, adding cold water to reach your desired consistency.
  3. Stir in the olive oil and sherry vinegar, season with salt.
  4. Chill at least 1 hour before serving.
  5. Serve cold, garnished with diced cucumber and a drizzle of olive oil.
