Marinated Olives & Feta

10 min total · 4 servings

Mixed olives and feta marinated in olive oil, lemon zest, and chili flakes.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 3g of carbs per serving, it fits comfortably within most low-carb diabetes meal plans, and its estimated glycemic index of ~0 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup kalamata olives
  • 4 oz low-sodium feta cheese
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano

Instructions

  1. Combine the olives and feta in a bowl.
  2. Toss with olive oil, lemon zest, chili flakes, and oregano.
  3. Marinate at least 1 hour before serving, or overnight for more flavor.
