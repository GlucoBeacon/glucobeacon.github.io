Nicaraguan Bean Soup

45 min total · 4 servings

A real, comforting Nicaraguan red bean soup with onion, bell pepper, and cumin -- everyday home cooking, naturally slow-digesting.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Beans are naturally low-GI and packed with soluble fiber, so this soup releases its carbohydrates gradually rather than in a quick spike. Relying entirely on the beans as the carb base, without rice or bread, makes this a genuinely gentle option for blood sugar.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups400 g dried red beans, soaked overnight
  • 11 onion, chopped
  • 11 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 3 cloves3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp1 tsp ground cumin
  • 6 cups1.4 L low-sodium vegetable broth

Instructions

  1. Drain the soaked beans and add them to a large pot with the broth, onion, bell pepper, garlic, and cumin.
  2. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 30-35 minutes until the beans are very tender.
  3. Mash some of the beans against the side of the pot to thicken the soup slightly.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste and serve hot.
