Enchiladas Verdes

35 min total · 3 servings

Corn tortillas rolled around shredded chicken, bathed in tangy tomatillo salsa verde and melted cheese -- a real Mexican family dinner staple.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Lean shredded chicken breast keeps the protein high relative to the carbs from the tortillas and sauce, and reduced-fat cheese trims saturated fat without losing the dish's real flavor.

Ingredients

  • 66 corn tortillas
  • 2 cups280 g shredded cooked chicken breast
  • 1.5 cups360 ml tomatillo salsa verde
  • 1/2 cup60 g shredded reduced-fat Mexican cheese blend
  • 2 tbsp30 ml crema
  • 2 tbsp20 g onion, diced

Instructions

  1. Warm the tortillas until pliable.
  2. Fill each tortilla with shredded chicken, roll, and place seam-down in a baking dish.
  3. Pour the salsa verde over the top and sprinkle with cheese.
  4. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 15 minutes until the cheese melts and bubbles.
  5. Top with crema and diced onion before serving.
