Grilled Fish with Colombian Salad

22 min total · 4 servings

A simple pan-seared fish fillet with lime and garlic, served with a fresh tomato-onion salad -- real Colombian coastal cooking, light and quick.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

A lean, pan-seared fish fillet with lime and garlic has essentially no carbs on its own, and pairing it with a fresh vegetable salad instead of rice or patacones (fried plantain) keeps the whole plate close to zero glycemic impact while still being a real, satisfying coastal-style dinner.

Ingredients

  • 44 white fish fillets (such as tilapia or snapper)
  • 3 tbsp45 ml fresh lime juice
  • 3 cloves3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp30 ml olive oil
  • 22 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/21/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup10 g fresh cilantro, chopped

Instructions

  1. Marinate the fish fillets in lime juice, garlic, salt, and pepper for 10 minutes.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat and sear the fish 3-4 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through.
  3. Meanwhile, toss the tomato, red onion, and cilantro together for the salad.
  4. Serve the fish topped with the fresh tomato-onion salad.
