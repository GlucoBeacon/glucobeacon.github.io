Light Fish in Coconut Sauce

35 min total · 4 servings

A real Dominican coastal dish -- fish simmered in a light coconut, tomato, and bell pepper sauce -- naturally low in carbs and rich in flavor.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

This dish is built on lean fish and a coconut-tomato sauce with no added starch, so its carbohydrate content stays naturally low. Using light coconut milk instead of the full-fat version keeps the real tropical richness of the sauce while trimming excess saturated fat.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb675 g white fish fillets (such as tilapia or mahi-mahi)
  • 11 onion, sliced
  • 11 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 22 tomatoes, diced
  • 3 cloves3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3/4 cup180 ml light coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp30 ml fresh lime juice

Instructions

  1. Season the fish with salt, pepper, and lime juice, and let it sit 10 minutes.
  2. Sauté the onion, bell pepper, and garlic in a large pan until soft, about 6 minutes.
  3. Add the tomato and cook 3 minutes more.
  4. Stir in the coconut milk and bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. Add the fish and simmer 10-12 minutes until just cooked through, then serve hot.
