Quinoa and Avocado Salad

30 min total · 4 servings

A real Andean-style salad built on quinoa, one of Ecuador's traditional highland grains, with creamy avocado, corn, and lime.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Quinoa is a whole grain with a moderate glycemic index and real fiber and protein content, unlike refined white rice. Pairing it with avocado's healthy fat and fiber slows digestion further, making this a naturally gentle option on blood sugar.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups370 g cooked quinoa, cooled
  • 11 avocado, diced
  • 1/2 cup75 g corn kernels
  • 11 tomato, diced
  • 1/41/4 red onion, finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp45 ml fresh lime juice
  • 2 tbsp30 ml olive oil

Instructions

  1. Combine the quinoa, avocado, corn, tomato, and red onion in a large bowl.
  2. Whisk together the lime juice and olive oil, and pour over the salad.
  3. Toss gently to combine, being careful not to mash the avocado.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste, and serve at room temperature or chilled.
