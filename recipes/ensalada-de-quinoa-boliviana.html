Bolivian Quinoa Salad

30 min total · 4 servings

A real Bolivian highland salad built on quinoa -- the grain Bolivia is famous for growing -- with tomato, corn, and lime.

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

Quinoa is a complete protein and a whole grain with a moderate glycemic index, unlike refined white rice or pasta. Serving it cold as a salad with fresh vegetables and lime makes for a naturally slow-digesting, satisfying dish that supports steady blood sugar.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups370 g cooked quinoa, cooled
  • 22 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 cup75 g corn kernels
  • 1/41/4 red onion, finely chopped
  • 1/4 cup10 g fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 3 tbsp45 ml fresh lime juice
  • 2 tbsp30 ml olive oil

Instructions

  1. Combine the quinoa, tomato, corn, and red onion in a large bowl.
  2. Whisk together the lime juice and olive oil, and pour over the salad.
  3. Toss gently to combine and stir in fresh cilantro.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste, and serve chilled or at room temperature.
