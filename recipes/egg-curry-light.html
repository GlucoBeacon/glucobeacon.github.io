Egg Curry, Light

Why this fits a diabetes-friendly diet

At 8g of carbs per serving, it fits comfortably within most low-carb diabetes meal plans, and its estimated glycemic index of ~15 means the carbs it does have tend to digest slowly rather than spiking blood sugar fast, and 14g of protein per serving also helps slow digestion and blunt any post-meal rise.

Instructions

  1. Peel the hard-boiled eggs and set aside.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and sauté the onion, garlic, and ginger until golden, about 6 minutes.
  3. Add the tomato, cumin powder, coriander powder, turmeric, and red chili powder, and cook 5-6 minutes until the tomato breaks down and the oil separates.
  4. Add a splash of water and simmer 5 minutes, then add the eggs and simmer 5 more minutes to soak up the flavor.
  5. Season with salt, garnish with cilantro, and serve hot.
